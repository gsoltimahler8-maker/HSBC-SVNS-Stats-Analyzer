# SVNS Stats Analyzer Brand Policy

Status: Active  
Adopted at: 2026-07-21

## Public name

```text
SVNS Stats Analyzer
```

## Positioning

SVNS Stats Analyzer is an independent, unofficial analytics tool.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or provided by World Rugby, HSBC, Rugby Australia, YouTube, or other rights holders.

## Rules

1. Do not use HSBC in the application name.
2. Do not use official World Rugby, HSBC, SVNS, Rugby Australia, or broadcaster logos without written permission.
3. Do not describe the application as official, approved, certified, partnered, sponsored, or provided unless a formal agreement exists.
4. Use original UI, original artwork, and original icons.
5. Use competition and organization names only to identify the subject of analysis and data sources.
6. Display a clear unofficial and non-affiliation notice.
7. Attribute data and video sources separately from the application brand.
8. Reassess naming and branding if a rights holder requests clarification or if commercial use begins.
