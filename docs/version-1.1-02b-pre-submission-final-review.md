## 1. Revision purpose

Strengthen the reason for opening the public prototype while keeping the first enquiry cooperative and non-commercial.

The revision makes two changes:

1. It explains the analytical value of the application more concretely.
2. It clarifies that the sender is not requesting confidential or restricted data.

It does not put possible publication withdrawal at the centre of the first enquiry.

---

## 2. Revised value proposition

The revised wording states that the application:

- helps users understand how performance changes across matches and tournaments;
- converts match-level team statistics into comparable indicators;
- covers results, scoring efficiency, attacking efficiency, discipline, defence and ball retention;
- supports comparison by tournament, opponent and match result; and
- follows changes over time.

This gives the recipient a clearer reason to open the public prototype without listing all 13 indicators in the enquiry itself.

---

## 3. Revised access-position statement

The initial enquiry now includes:

> At this stage, I am not seeking access to any confidential or restricted data. I am simply trying to understand whether an official route exists and what conditions would apply. I would, of course, be happy to adjust the prototype if necessary.

This distinguishes a request for guidance from a demand for restricted data access.

---

## 4. Wording held back for follow-up

The following sentence is not included in the initial enquiry:

> If the current public prototype does not align with the applicable conditions, I would be happy to adjust it or take it offline.

It is reserved for a reply in which World Rugby raises a concern, identifies incompatible conditions or asks what corrective action would be taken.
